What can I do in the live preview?

Play all 300 demo sounds, search names and analysed descriptors, hop along similarity links, change layouts and colours, filter by key or tempo, collect a basket, and explore Kit, Sketchpad and exports. This is the desktop viewer itself.

What needs the desktop app?

Mapping your own folders, running the sound model for new audio or microphone searches, watching library changes, native DAW dragging and Ableton Link need the desktop app. Browser similarity uses precomputed demo links. MIDI input and recording depend on browser support and permissions.

Are these real sounds?

Yes. This is the app's bundled 300-sound CC0 demo, from Freesound creators via FSL10K and FSD50K. The website uses MP3 previews; the desktop app uses your original files. See creator credits ↗

Can I still explore the big Freesound map?

The original interactive library is still here, including search, filters, similarity links, the basket and downloads. Open the Freesound map ↗
